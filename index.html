Privacy Policy

  • MyChoice does not collect your personal data and does not send it to any server. Nothing is shared with anyone.
  • Your habit names, start dates, goals, notes and savings amounts stay only on your device, inside the app and its widgets.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, and the app does not connect to the internet.
  • Milestone notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.
  • A backup file is created only when you export it, and it is saved where you choose. The app never uploads it.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Backups of your device made by Apple (iCloud or a computer) follow your own Apple settings.
